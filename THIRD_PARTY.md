- Apple graphics drivers, OpenCL/Metal compiler components and associated
  resources are Apple/Intel-derived binaries, with modifications for the Xe
  stack. Their existing copyright and resource notices are retained. They are
  not covered by this repository's packaging-tool license.
- AppleIntelTGLCompat source is maintained in
  [WhateverXe](https://github.com/b00t0x/WhateverXe/tree/main/AppleIntelTGLCompat),
  with its BSD-2-Clause source notices. The package includes its compiled form.
- AMFIPass 1.4.1 Mod is supplied as an unchanged separate ZIP. It derives from
  AMFIPass distributed by
  [Dortania](https://github.com/dortania/OpenCore-Legacy-Patcher); the modified trust anchor
  matches this package's signing chain. See [source information](docs/SOURCES.md).
